- UIL -

UIL (and other competitions) are competitions asses a team's skill at computer science (usually Java).
Competitions often of a multiple choice test and open-ended questions.
teams are up to 3 members, and competitions are against other Texan schools' teams.

District: Friday, March 28, 2025
Schedule:

  • 10:00 - 10:30: Roll call
  • 10:30 - 11:15: Multiple choice text
  • 11:15 - 12:15: Grading and lunch
  • 12:30 - 12:55: Computer setup and dry run
  • 1:00 - 3:00: Programming contest
  • 3:00 - 3:30: Final results
  • 3:30 - 4:00: Awards

- Best of Texas -

Best of Texas is a multiple choice test taken during computer science class to asses your knowledge of Java.
Tests are scored and compared over multiple schools in Texas.

We will normally give these on the final week of the month.
Schedule:

  • Round 1: November 1, 2025 - November 30, 2025
  • Round 2: January 1, 2026 - January 31, 2026
  • Round 3: February 1, 2026 - February 28, 2026
